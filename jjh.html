MOUSE

The mouse is a handheld input device that allows users to interact with a computer's graphical user interface. It typically has buttons and a scroll wheel, enabling users to navigate, select, and manipulate objects on the screen. Mice can be optical or laser-based, offering different levels of precision and performance. They come in various designs, from basic models to advanced gaming mice with customizable buttons and high DPI settings. The invention of the mouse is credited to Douglas Engelbart in the 1960s, revolutionizing how people interact with computers. Today, mice are indispensable tools for productivity and entertainment.
